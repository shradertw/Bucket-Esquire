# Bucket-Esquire
Play as a Bucket lawyer.

**Play it:** https://shradertw.github.io/Bucket-Esquire/

You are Ms. Bucket, attorney at law. Your clients are tools and household things, accused of small crimes. Each case is randomly one of two kinds:

- **Catch the lies**: a witness testifies. Pick a statement you think is false, pick the evidence that contradicts it, and object. Strike every lie to win. Some statements are true, and objecting to them costs you.
- **Build the story**: answer three questions for your client so their story fits every piece of evidence.

The judge allows three mistakes per case. After that, it's Guilty.

Everything is in `index.html` (no build step). Open it in a browser to play locally.
